AIONOS ENTERPRISE — FINANCE DEPARTMENT
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: INVOICE PROCESSING & MISMATCH RESOLUTION
Version 2.4 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: CFO Office

1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This SOP governs the end-to-end invoice processing lifecycle, with specific
provisions for automated detection and resolution of invoice mismatches.
All AI-driven resolutions must comply with this policy.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. INVOICE MISMATCH THRESHOLDS & ESCALATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 AUTO-APPROVE THRESHOLD (No Human Required)
   - Mismatch ≤ 2% of PO value AND ≤ $500 absolute: System may auto-approve.
   - Agent must document reason and notify AP team via email.

2.2 SENIOR AP ANALYST APPROVAL REQUIRED
   - Mismatch > 2% but ≤ 10% of PO value OR between $500–$5,000 absolute.
   - Response required within 4 business hours.
   - Agent must provide: invoice detail, PO detail, mismatch reason, supplier history.

2.3 CFO APPROVAL REQUIRED (High-Risk Escalation)
   - Mismatch > 10% of PO value OR > $5,000 absolute difference.
   - Duplicate invoice detected.
   - Missing PO reference on invoice.
   - Currency mismatch (multi-currency invoices).
   - Response required within 24 business hours.

2.4 AUTOMATIC REJECTION CRITERIA (Agent May Auto-Reject)
   - Invoice submitted more than 90 days after delivery.
   - Supplier not on approved vendor list.
   - Invoice amount exceeds supplier credit limit.
   - VAT arithmetic error > 0.01%.

3. DUPLICATE INVOICE DETECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- A duplicate is defined as: same supplier + same PO number + amount within 1%.
- All duplicates must be escalated immediately to CFO regardless of amount.
- Supplier must be notified via email within 2 business hours.
- Payment must be frozen until resolution.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. PAYMENT TERMS ENFORCEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Net 15: Payment within 15 days of invoice approval.
- Net 30: Standard terms — payment within 30 days.
- Net 45/60: Extended terms require CFO countersignature on contract.
- Early payment discount (2/10 Net 30): If supplier offers 2% discount for
  payment within 10 days, AP agent may recommend early payment.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. SUPPLIER CREDIT LIMIT POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Invoices that would cause total outstanding payables to exceed the supplier
  credit limit must be escalated to Procurement and CFO.
- Credit limits are reviewed quarterly by the Procurement team.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Every automated action taken by the Finance Agent must be logged in the
  immutable audit trail with: timestamp, action type, invoice ID, agent decision,
  and policy reference cited.
- All CFO-level approvals must be countersigned within the ERP system.
- Audit logs are retained for 7 years per SOX compliance requirements.
