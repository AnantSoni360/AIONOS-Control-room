1. SLA BREACH DEFINITION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A supplier SLA breach occurs when a shipment is not delivered within the
contractually agreed number of days from order confirmation date.

SLA Breach Severity Classification:
  - MINOR BREACH: 1–3 days delayed — Agent may auto-notify supplier.
  - MODERATE BREACH: 4–7 days delayed — Penalty invoice generated, requires
    Procurement Manager review.
  - MAJOR BREACH: 8–14 days delayed — COO must approve penalty enforcement.
    Supplier placed on watchlist.
  - CRITICAL BREACH: > 14 days delayed OR cargo value > $100,000 affected —
    Immediate COO + Legal review. Contract termination may be initiated.

2. AUTOMATED RESOLUTION ACTIONS (Agent Authority)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 MINOR BREACH (1–3 days)
   - Agent sends automated breach notification email to supplier.
   - Agent logs breach in Operations dashboard.
   - No penalty invoice generated for first minor breach per quarter.
   - Second minor breach: 50% standard penalty applies.
   - Third minor breach in a quarter: Treated as moderate breach.

2.2 MODERATE BREACH (4–7 days)
   - Agent generates draft penalty invoice.
   - Agent sends formal breach letter to supplier with cure period of 48 hours.
   - Penalty calculation: Days delayed × contract penalty_per_day.
   - Cap at contract max_penalty.
   - Procurement Manager must approve penalty invoice before issuing.

2.3 MAJOR BREACH (8–14 days)
   - Agent escalates to COO with full incident report.
   - Penalty invoice sent automatically to supplier (no additional approval needed).
   - Supplier placed on 90-day enhanced monitoring.
   - Agent notifies downstream teams of supply impact.
   - COO must respond within 4 business hours.

2.4 CRITICAL BREACH (> 14 days or high-value cargo)
   - AGENT MUST ESCALATE — no autonomous action permitted.
   - COO + Legal team review within 2 business hours.
   - Emergency procurement alternatives identified by agent.
   - Contract termination clause review initiated.

3. PENALTY ENFORCEMENT POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Penalty = min(days_delayed × penalty_per_day, max_penalty)
- Penalties are deducted from the next payable invoice to the supplier.
- Disputed penalties: Supplier has 10 business days to dispute via formal notice.
- Dispute resolution: Joint review by Procurement and Finance teams.
- Force Majeure clause: Penalties waived if delay caused by documented acts of
  God, government action, or port strike (supplier must notify within 24h of event).

4. SUPPLIER WATCHLIST POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Added to watchlist: 2 moderate breaches or 1 major breach in 6 months.
- Watchlist review: Quarterly by Procurement Committee.
- Watchlist consequences: No new contract awards; existing contracts not renewed.
- Removal from watchlist: 6 months breach-free performance + formal remediation plan.
- Blacklist (permanent): 3 major breaches or 1 critical breach in 12 months.

5. AUDIT REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- All breach events, notifications, and penalty actions logged in Operations
  audit trail with shipment ID, contract ID, and SLA reference.
- Monthly SLA compliance report generated by Operations team.
- Supplier scorecards updated within 48 hours of any breach resolution.
