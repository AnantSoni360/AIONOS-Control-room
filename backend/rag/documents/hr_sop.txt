AIONOS ENTERPRISE — HR DEPARTMENT
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING & BLOCKER RESOLUTION
Version 3.1 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: Chief People Officer

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. ONBOARDING TIMELINE SLA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Day 0 (Start Date): Laptop, email, and building access must be provisioned.
- Day 1: HR portal registration, payroll setup, benefits enrollment.
- Day 3: All legal documents (NDA, employment contract, IP agreement) signed.
- Day 5: VPN, all software licenses, and system access granted.
- Day 14: Onboarding officially complete. Manager signs off in HRIS.

2. BLOCKER ESCALATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 IT BLOCKERS
   - Laptop not provisioned by Day 0: IT Helpdesk must resolve within 4 hours.
     If unresolved after 4h, escalate to IT Manager.
   - VPN/software license issue: IT has 8 hours to resolve from ticket creation.
   - System access failures: IT Helpdesk SLA is 2 business hours.
   - Agent action: Create IT ticket, notify IT Helpdesk, set reminder for SLA.

2.2 LEGAL BLOCKERS
   - Unsigned NDA or employment contract after Day 1: HR must follow up directly.
   - Background check delay > 5 business days: Escalate to background check vendor
     and notify Legal team. Employee may begin work with provisional clearance.
   - IP agreement unsigned after Day 2: Legal Counsel must be notified.
   - Agent action: Send DocuSign reminder, notify Legal team, log in HRIS.

2.3 MANAGER BLOCKERS
   - Manager approval pending > 24 hours: Send escalation email to manager.
   - Manager out of office: Delegate approval authority to department head.
   - Agent action: Identify backup approver, reroute approval, log action.

2.4 FACILITIES BLOCKERS
   - Access card not issued by Day 0: Facilities team notified with 2h SLA.
   - Desk not assigned: Facilities Manager must respond within 4 hours.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. CRITICAL ESCALATION (CPO REQUIRED)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Employee blocked for more than 5 business days.
- Background check reveals a disqualifying finding.
- Employee reports hostile environment or safety concern.
- Onboarding for C-suite or VP-level hire experiencing any blocker.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- I-9 verification must be completed by Day 3 (US employees).
- GDPR data processing consent must be obtained before storing EU employee data.
- All onboarding actions must be logged in the HRIS audit trail.
- Background check vendor: HireRight (primary), Sterling (backup).
- Background check consent: Employee must provide written consent before check begins.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. PROBATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Standard probation: 90 days from start date.
- During probation, either party may terminate with 2 weeks notice.
- Probation may be extended by manager with CPO approval (max 30-day extension).
- Extension requires documented performance concerns submitted to HR.
