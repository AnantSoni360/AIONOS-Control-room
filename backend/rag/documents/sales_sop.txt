STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: DEAL MANAGEMENT & STALLED DEAL RECOVERY
Version 1.8 | Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: Chief Revenue Officer

1. DEAL STALL DEFINITION & THRESHOLDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A deal is classified as "stalled" when there has been no logged CRM activity
(call, email, meeting, or note) for the following periods by stage:

  - Prospecting: > 14 days without activity
  - Qualification: > 14 days without activity
  - Proposal: > 21 days without activity
  - Negotiation: > 30 days without activity

Any deal in any stage with > 45 days of inactivity is CRITICAL.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. AUTOMATED RECOVERY ACTIONS (Agent May Execute)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 STAGE 1 — AUTOMATED NUDGE (Days 14–29 stalled)
   - Agent sends automated follow-up email to assigned rep.
   - Agent logs a "follow-up reminder" task in CRM.
   - Agent updates deal "last_activity_date" after logging the task.

2.2 STAGE 2 — MANAGER ALERT (Days 30–44 stalled)
   - Agent notifies Sales Manager via email with deal summary.
   - Agent schedules a review meeting between rep and manager.
   - Agent may recommend deal stage downgrade if probability < 30%.

2.3 STAGE 3 — CRO ESCALATION (> 45 days stalled, CRITICAL)
   - Agent escalates to CRO with full deal brief.
   - Requires human decision: continue, reassign rep, or close lost.
   - Agent must provide: deal value, contact info, stall reason, win probability.
   - Agent MUST NOT change deal status without CRO approval at this stage.

3. DEAL REASSIGNMENT POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Reassignment requires Sales Manager approval for deals < $50,000.
- Reassignment requires CRO approval for deals ≥ $50,000.
- Enterprise deals (≥ $100,000): VP of Sales must co-approve reassignment.
- Agent must notify both old and new reps, and update CRM within 1 hour.

4. RISK SCORING GUIDELINES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
High-Risk deal indicators (agent must flag):
  - Expected close date has passed (deal overdue)
  - Win probability < 20%
  - Budget freeze confirmed by prospect
  - Primary champion has left the prospect company
  - More than 2 competitor evaluations in progress

Low-Risk indicators (agent may handle autonomously):
  - Stall due to prospect scheduling conflicts
  - Awaiting legal review with known timeline
  - Holiday/seasonal delays within expected range

5. CLOSING PROTOCOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Closed Won: Revenue recognized in current quarter if contract signed by
  last business day of quarter. Agent updates CRM status immediately.
- Closed Lost: Agent must log loss reason (mandatory). Sales Manager must
  review within 5 business days for win/loss analysis.
- Discounts > 20%: Require VP of Sales approval before contract execution.
- Discounts > 35%: Require CRO approval.
